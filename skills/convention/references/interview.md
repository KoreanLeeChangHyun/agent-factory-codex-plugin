# Interview Capability Convention

## Capability boundary

Interview reduces a material information gap between AI and the Human through
adaptive elicitation in the current Main conversation. It produces a Processed
Document by default and does not independently promote its result to
Specification truth.

Interview is not a public distributed Skill or managed Exec role. Main owns its
conduct under `skills/agent/`; this reference owns its durable information and
Human-authority constraints.

## Information and authority

- Use Interview only when the available context cannot resolve a material gap
  in Human knowledge, requirements, or decisions.
- Do not replace an ordinary answer with an interview, survey external people,
  or launch an Exec Agent to impersonate the Human.
- Keep direct Human statements and explicit decisions distinct from Explorer
  evidence, Main paraphrases and interpretations, assumptions,
  contradictions, and unresolved gaps.
- Silence, ambiguity, and lack of objection are not approval.
- The Human may skip, defer, correct, narrow, or stop the Interview.
- Do not solicit passwords, tokens, private keys, or other secrets. Ask for
  other sensitive information only when necessary and authorized.
- Keep the result in the conversation unless the Human requests a target or an
  already-authorized owning workflow requires an artifact.

Main may pause or sequence Interview while Work applies the Explorer capability
to gather external background, then integrate that evidence and resume the
Human conversation. Work never asks Interview questions of or impersonates the
Human on Explorer's behalf.
