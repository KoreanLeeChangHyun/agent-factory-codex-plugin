# Explicit Human Input

## Decision boundary

- Explicit means stated by the Human or unambiguously resolved by an accepted
  Specification: requirements, choices, scope, authority and acceptance criteria.
- For missing required decisions, ask and wait before the affected action.
  Never invent/default a decision or present an assumption as one.
- Silence, ambiguity, convention, precedent, likely preference and lack of objection
  are not decisions. Inspection supplies observable facts, not Human authority.

## Role responsibilities

- **Main:** ask and wait for required Human decisions.
- **Work/Verification:** report gaps to Main; neither ask directly nor proceed
  through the affected decision.
- **Explorer:** gather facts; never convert evidence into choices or interview/impersonate Humans.
- **Interview:** Main alone; distinguish statements, evidence, interpretations and gaps.
- Preserve existing roles, safety, authority, provenance and graph contracts.
