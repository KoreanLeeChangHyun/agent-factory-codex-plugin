# Interview Conduct

## Frame the interview

Before asking questions, identify the outcome the interview should enable,
what is already known, which gaps materially affect that outcome, applicable
constraints, and a practical stopping condition. Briefly share that frame with
the Human when it helps them understand the scope or correct a mistaken
premise.

Do not start an interview when the available context already supports the
ordinary answer, the request is to survey external people, or the only gap is
one trivial clarification. Route external evidence needs to an authorized
managed Explorer or gathering workflow instead of asking the Human to stand in
for an unknown source. Main may pause or sequence the Interview while Explorer
gathers necessary background, then resume after integrating the returned
evidence. Explorer never asks Interview questions of or impersonates the Human.

## Conduct adaptively

- Ask only questions whose answers can materially reduce the identified gap.
- Prefer a small, focused question or tightly related set over a long batch.
- Use neutral wording. Avoid leading, compound, repetitive, and
  curiosity-only questions.
- Adapt follow-ups to what the Human actually says; do not force answers into a
  predetermined questionnaire.
- Allow the Human to skip, defer, correct, narrow, or stop without treating
  that choice as approval or a negative answer.
- Ask for sensitive information only when it is necessary to the stated
  purpose and within the Human's authorization. Never solicit passwords,
  tokens, private keys, or other secrets.

Track the epistemic status of what emerges. Keep direct Human statements and
explicit Human decisions distinct from Explorer evidence, Main paraphrases and
interpretations, assumptions, contradictions, and unresolved gaps. Silence,
ambiguity, and lack of objection are not approval.

Reflect back any consequential interpretation before relying on it. When two
answers conflict, surface the conflict neutrally and ask the smallest question
needed to resolve it; if it remains unresolved, preserve both positions and
their status.

## Stop and present the result

End when the defined gap is sufficiently reduced for the stated outcome, the
Human stops, or the remaining gap depends on external evidence or a deferred
Human-owned decision. Do not continue merely to make the interview feel
complete.

Present a concise synthesis shaped to the conversation rather than a rigid
document template. Make consequential Human statements and decisions
traceable, label Agent interpretations and assumptions, and keep
contradictions and unresolved gaps visible. State why the interview stopped
when the remaining gap affects downstream work.

The synthesis is processed information. Keep it in the conversation by
default. Write it only to an explicitly requested target or through an
already-authorized owning workflow. A later explicit Specification request may
refine accepted outcomes into paired Human- and AI-facing representations, but
must preserve provenance, distinctions, contradictions, and unresolved state.
